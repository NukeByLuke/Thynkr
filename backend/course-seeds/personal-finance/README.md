# Personal Finance Mastery Course Materials

This folder contains 8 comprehensive modules covering all aspects of personal finance from a Canadian perspective.

## Course Overview

**Course Title:** Personal Finance Mastery
**Level:** Beginner to Intermediate
**Duration:** 8 modules
**Target Audience:** Canadian students and young professionals

## Module List

1. **The Psychology of Wealth and Goal Architecture**
   - Money mindset transformation
   - PACED decision-making model
   - SMART goals framework
   - Compound effect principles

2. **Advanced Budgeting Frameworks and Cash Flow**
   - 50/30/20 Rule
   - Zero-Based Budgeting
   - Pay Yourself First methodology
   - Cash flow management

3. **Banking Architecture and Liquidity Management**
   - Chequing vs. High-Yield Savings Accounts
   - Emergency fund strategy
   - Cash Management Accounts
   - Banking automation

4. **Credit Engineering and Debt Elimination**
   - Credit score components (Canadian FICO)
   - Debt Snowball vs. Debt Avalanche
   - Good debt vs. Bad debt
   - Credit building strategies

5. **Canadian Tax Fundamentals and Optimization**
   - Marginal vs. Effective tax rates
   - Student tax credits and deductions
   - T4, T2202, and Notice of Assessment
   - Legal tax minimization strategies

6. **Investment Vehicles: TFSAs, RRSPs, and Beyond**
   - Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA)
   - Registered Retirement Savings Plan (RRSP)
   - ETFs and Index Funds
   - Asset allocation by age

7. **Risk Management and the Insurance Safety Net**
   - Health, disability, and life insurance
   - Auto and tenant's insurance
   - Deductibles and coverage optimization
   - Insurance vs. Emergency fund

8. **Long-Term Horizons: Retirement and Estate Planning**
   - 4% Rule for retirement withdrawals
   - CPP and OAS benefits
   - Will and Power of Attorney
   - Estate tax planning
